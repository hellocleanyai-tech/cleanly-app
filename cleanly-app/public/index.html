FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started with Cleanly AI.

What file formats does Cleanly AI support?

Comma-separated .csv files, saved as UTF-8. Excel, Google Sheets and every major CRM can export this format directly — choose “CSV” when you export. Files with a different extension are rejected before they upload.

How long does AI cleaning take?

Most files finish in under a minute. Large lists take a few minutes. You don't have to wait on the page — your dashboard shows the live status of every upload, and the download appears as soon as the file is ready.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes. Cancel from Account → Manage billing in two clicks, with no email required. You keep full access until the end of the period you've already paid for.

Is my uploaded data secure?

Files are encrypted in transit and at rest, and stored in a private per-account folder. Downloads use short-lived signed links that expire after ten minutes. No other customer can reach your files, and we never sell or share your contact data.

Do unused uploads roll over to the next month?

No. Your upload allowance resets on the first day of each month (UTC) and unused uploads don't carry over. If you regularly run out, moving up a plan costs less than the time spent rationing files.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan later?

Anytime, in both directions. Change plans from your dashboard and the new limits apply immediately — billing is prorated automatically, so you only pay the difference.
