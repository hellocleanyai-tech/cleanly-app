Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

This policy explains what Cleanly AI collects, why, and what happens to the contact lists you upload. This is a starting template — have it reviewed by a lawyer before launch, particularly for GDPR, since you process personal data belonging to third parties on your customers' behalf.

Who we are

Cleanly AI provides an automated CSV cleaning service. For the contact data inside the files you upload, you are the data controller and we act as your data processor.

What we collect

  • Account data — your email address, and your Google account identifier if you sign in with Google.
  • Subscription data — your plan, status and billing period. Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy; we never see or store your card details.
  • Uploaded files — the CSV files you upload and the cleaned versions we produce.
  • Usage data — upload counts and timestamps, used to enforce plan limits.

How uploaded files are handled

Files are stored in a private per-account folder, encrypted in transit and at rest. Downloads use short-lived signed links that expire after ten minutes. Access is restricted at the database level so no other customer can reach your files. We do not sell, share or use your contact data to train models.

Processors we use

  • Supabase — authentication, database and file storage.
  • Lemon Squeezy — payments, subscriptions and invoicing (merchant of record).
  • Netlify — website hosting and server functions.

Retention

Uploaded and cleaned files are retained while your account is active. Contact us to delete specific files or your entire account, and we will remove your data within 30 days, except where we must keep billing records for tax purposes.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to processing. Email us to exercise any of these rights.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@cleanly.ai
